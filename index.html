📚 Space Science Learning Corner

Mass: How heavy your spacecraft is. Your rocket must be able to carry it.

Power: The energy needed to operate your spacecraft and instruments.

Budget: The money available to build and operate your mission.

Data: The information collected by your scientific instruments.

Communications: How much information your spacecraft can send back to Earth.

Orbit: The path a spacecraft follows around a planet or another object.

Mission lifetime: How long your spacecraft is expected to operate.

Trade-off: Improving one part of a mission may make another part harder. Good engineers balance everything!

This game uses simplified educational values, not real spacecraft engineering calculations.
